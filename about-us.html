About Cloud Social Work: NDIS therapeutic supports and social work in Wollongong, Nowra and Sydney

Cloud Social Work is a person-centred practice led by Michael McGowan. We provide Therapeutic Supports for the NDIS and Social Work services across the Illawarra, Nowra and Sydney, with a focus on sustainable, long-term change.

Who we are

Cloud Social Work exists to provide compassionate, person-centred support for people in the Illawarra, including Wollongong, as well as Nowra and Sydney. The practice is run by Michael McGowan, and the work is grounded in social work values: empathy, empowerment and integrity.

The services we provide are clear. We offer Therapeutic Supports for the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) for NDIA Managed, Plan Managed and Self Managed participants. We also provide Social Work services. Those two areas of work are the foundation of the practice, and they are what you can expect when you get in touch.

Our aim is not only to help in the moment, but to help people build a foundation for lasting, positive change. That means taking time to understand each person, developing support that fits their goals, and working in a way that is respectful, transparent and accountable.

If you are considering Cloud Social Work for yourself, a family member, or a participant you support, the most useful first step is to tell us a little about what you need. You can use the enquiry form on this page, visit the contact page, or book in when you are ready to start an application.

Therapeutic Supports for NDIS participants

Therapeutic Supports are a core part of Cloud Social Work. We work with NDIS participants who are NDIA Managed, Plan Managed or Self Managed. The purpose of this work is to help people build capacity, work toward their functional goals, and receive support that is tailored to their plan.

Sessions are personalised. That can include support with emotional regulation, mental health challenges and behavioural issues, using evidence-based therapeutic approaches. The homepage of this site describes our methodology as rooted in the NDIS framework, so that support is compliant, effective and designed around the individual rather than a one-size-fits-all program.

If you are looking for Therapeutic Supports in Wollongong, the wider Illawarra, Nowra or Sydney, we welcome an enquiry so we can talk through whether we are a good fit. We will not claim a service we do not provide, and we will be straightforward about what therapeutic support can involve.

Social Work services

Alongside NDIS Therapeutic Supports, Cloud Social Work provides Social Work services. Social workers assess needs, develop care plans and coordinate support so that people are not left to navigate complex systems on their own.

This work can include guidance, case management and advocacy. It may involve identifying barriers, connecting with community resources, and building a support network around a person. It can also include crisis intervention or longer-term case management, depending on what is needed.

Advocacy is part of this role. Cloud Social Work’s approach includes standing with people to address systemic barriers and to promote equity and inclusion. That does not mean promising an outcome we cannot control. It means doing the careful, practical work of helping people be heard and helping them access the support they are entitled to.

How we work

The practice is person-centred. That means the starting point is the person in front of us — their goals, their circumstances, and what matters to them — rather than a standard package of activities.

We use evidence-based practices and keep professional development as a standing part of the work, so that support stays current and carefully delivered. Social work here includes assessment, planning and coordination. Therapeutic support is shaped around NDIS goals and delivered in a way that is practical and respectful.

Our core values are empathy, empowerment and integrity. Empathy means approaching every person with understanding and making sure they feel heard. Empowerment means providing tools and support so people can take a greater role in their own lives. Integrity means operating with transparency, honesty and accountability.

Work may take place in indoor settings or, where it suits the person and the work, in outdoor or community settings. The important part is that the setting supports the work, not that a particular activity is offered as a packaged program.
